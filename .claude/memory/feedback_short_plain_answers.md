When reporting analysis or design options, lead with a short plain-language rundown, one item at a time, and give a verdict per item. Hold the evidence back unless asked.

**Why:** on 2026-11-27 I answered "have a think about issue 1445 and some possible design choices" with an ~800-word structured write-up (data section, five design items, gaps, build order, caveats). The user replied "right too mant words, run through 1through 5 one at atime, eli5". The five-item version at two or three short sentences each got an immediate decision on every item.

**How to apply:** for each option say what it is, why it matters and build / don't build, in everyday words with no internal identifiers. Numbers only when they decide something. This is my reading of one exchange, so it covers summaries and design discussions; code review detail and debugging reports may still need the specifics.
